Questions

Before your bot goes live

Does Crypto Robot backtest my bot on historical data?

No. Crypto Robot is the live step that comes after a backtest. You backtest with standard tools, such as a charting platform's strategy tester or a Python library, then run the bot here on live prices with a virtual $10,000. The backtesting guide shows the full method.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Opening an account asks for no card and no deposit.

Can I win or lose real money?

No. Each bot trades a virtual balance. There is no deposit, no withdrawal and no payout: the only thing at stake is your strategy's record.

How long does a live test last?

Level 1 lasts between 5 and 10 days. Level 2 needs at least 5 days and has no end date. Level 3 has neither a minimum nor a deadline.

What stops a test early?

A loss of 5% in a single day, or a total drawdown of 10%. Either one ends the challenge on the spot.

Can I test more than one bot?

Yes. Each bot you set up gets its own virtual $10,000 balance, so you can compare versions of a strategy side by side.
