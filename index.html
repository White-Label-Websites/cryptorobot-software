How it works

From strategy to verdict

  1. Set up your bot

    Choose a market and the rules for entering and leaving trades. A grid, trend or mean-reversion idea is enough to start.

  2. Let it trade live prices

    The bot runs on real-time crypto prices with a virtual $10,000, and every limit is checked for you.

  3. Climb or rebuild

    Reach the target to unlock the next level. Break a limit and the run shows where the strategy failed.

Questions

Before you launch

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You can set up a bot from simple rules. If you already code your own bots, you can test the same logic here on live prices.

Is it really free?

Yes. Signing up asks for no card, no deposit and no payment of any kind.

Is any real money traded?

No. Every bot trades a virtual balance of $10,000. Prices come from live crypto markets, the money does not.

Why are there drawdown limits?

A bot that wins big once and then loses half the account is not a good bot. The -5% daily and -10% maximum drawdown limits reward strategies that stay under control.

What happens when my bot breaks a limit?

The run ends immediately. You can see which day and which trades caused it, adjust the strategy and start a new bot.

Can I test a bot written with ChatGPT or another AI?

Yes. Read our guides on the ChatGPT trading bot and AI crypto trading bots, then run yours here.
